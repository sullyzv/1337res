Display controls

Monitor controls

Find disabled displays, identify active screens by number, and switch monitors on or off while keeping at least one screen active.

Global hotkeys

Bind a shortcut to each saved profile and use it while 1337res runs in your system tray.

Custom modes

Enter a width, height, and refresh rate. 1337res checks the mode with your graphics driver before applying it.

15-second rollback

Confirm a display change before the timer runs out. You can also restore a monitor’s previous resolution from the app or tray.

Set up your displays.

1337res stores your profiles and hotkeys on your PC.

  1. Run 1337res

    Download and open the Windows executable.

  2. Choose what’s active

    Open Monitors to enable or disable displays. Open Display to choose a resolution and refresh rate.

  3. Save a profile

    Give it a name, assign a shortcut, and keep 1337res running in the tray.
